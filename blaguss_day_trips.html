Discover the Surroundings

Budapest's surroundings hide some real gems waiting to be discovered. Following the Danube north from the Hungarian capital, you'll reach the scenic Danube Bend, once part of the ancient Roman limes. Today, this picturesque region is dotted with charming towns, each offering its own unique atmosphere.

Szentendre feels like an open-air museum, with colourful streets and small shops spilling out onto the pavements, inviting you to browse as you stroll by. Located right on the banks of the Danube, it's perfect for a relaxed walk along the river. Visegrád is famous for its breathtaking views: a refreshing hike through wooded trails leads to the 13th-century citadel, where you can admire the sweeping curve of the river and the distant Börzsöny Hills. Esztergom, the former capital of Hungary, impresses visitors with its rich history, while Gödöllő is home to the magnificent 18th-century Royal Palace, once a favourite retreat of Queen Elisabeth, known as Sisi.

To make your stay even more memorable, consider a wine tour in Etyek, where you can explore Hungarian wines, local traditions, and countryside life. For a truly unique experience, visit the Lázár Equestrian Park, offering delicious food and drinks, a scenic carriage ride through the forest, and an exciting horse show — an enjoyable program even for those who are not horse enthusiasts. If you feel inspired to explore beyond the capital, the Blaguss team will be happy to organise the perfect excursion for you.

Insider Knowledge

Lights, Camera, Action: Become the Star!

Pro Tip from our team

Did you know that Hungary is a favourite filming location for major international productions? The Etyek film studio has hosted numerous blockbuster movies, making it one of the country's most exciting cinematic hubs.

Here, guests don't just watch the magic — they become part of it, and can be stars for a day. During this unique experience, they can create their own short film, try professional make-up techniques, or step into the role of a dubbing actor, making it a fun and interactive program full of unforgettable moments — an excellent addition to a wine tasting program.
